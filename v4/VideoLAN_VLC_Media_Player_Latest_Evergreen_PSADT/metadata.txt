**VLC Media Player** is a free, open-source, cross-platform media player developed by the VideoLAN project. It plays a wide range of audio and video formats without requiring separate codec installations.

## Key Features

* Playback of virtually all common audio and video formats including MP4, MKV, AVI, MP3, FLAC, and many others
* Built-in codec library with no dependency on external codec packs
* Streaming playback from network sources, DVDs, Blu-rays, and webcams
* Hardware-accelerated video decoding for reduced CPU usage
* Subtitle support including SSA, SRT, and DVD subtitle formats
* Audio and video filters, equaliser, and colour correction
* Media conversion and transcoding
* Playlist management and media library
* Cross-platform: available on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS

VLC is deployed as a standard media player in environments where users need to play video files from a variety of sources without relying on operating system codecs.
