Description:
**Microsoft Power Apps** is the Windows front door to the Power Apps your organisation has built or shared with you, such as expenses, timesheets, inspections and event registration apps.

The latest version is downloaded from the Microsoft Store at install time and provisioned for all users on the device. The Microsoft Store keeps it up to date afterwards.
