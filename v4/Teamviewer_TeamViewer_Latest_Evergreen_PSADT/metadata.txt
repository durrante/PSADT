**TeamViewer** is a remote access and remote support application developed by TeamViewer SE. It allows IT staff to connect to, control and troubleshoot devices over the internet without requiring a VPN, and supports unattended access to servers and workstations.

## Key Features

* Attended and unattended remote control of Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS devices
* Cross-platform file transfer between the local and remote device
* Remote printing, session recording and multi-monitor support
* Wake-on-LAN, remote reboot and automatic reconnection after restart
* Built-in chat, voice and video conferencing for supporting end users
* Device grouping, access control and managed device policies via the Management Console
* End-to-end encryption with optional two-factor authentication and conditional access
* Compatible with currently supported Windows operating systems

TeamViewer is deployed in organisations to provide IT support to remote and home-based staff, and to administer unattended devices such as servers, kiosks and meeting room systems.
