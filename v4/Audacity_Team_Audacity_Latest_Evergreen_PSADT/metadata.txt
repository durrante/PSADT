**Audacity** is a free, open-source audio editor and recorder developed by the Audacity Team. It is widely used for recording, editing, and processing audio on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Key Features

* Multi-track audio recording and editing
* Import and export of a wide range of audio formats including MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, and AIFF
* Non-destructive editing with unlimited undo history
* Built-in effects including noise reduction, equalisation, compression, and normalisation
* Support for VST, LV2, and AU plugins
* Spectrogram view for frequency analysis
* Batch processing via macros

Audacity is used in environments such as education, podcasting, voice recording, and basic audio post-production.
