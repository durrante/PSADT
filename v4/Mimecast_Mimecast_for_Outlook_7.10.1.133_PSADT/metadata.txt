Description:
**Mimecast for Outlook** is a Microsoft Outlook add-in developed by Mimecast that integrates Mimecast's cloud-based email security, archiving, and continuity services directly into the Outlook client.

## Key Features

* Access to the Mimecast email archive with fast search capability from within Outlook
* Export of archived messages back into Outlook folders
* Large File Send for sending attachments up to 2 GB via secure links
* Secure Messaging for sending encrypted messages to external recipients
* Targeted Threat Protection controls including safe URL checking and attachment sandboxing
* Content control and data leak prevention policy enforcement
* Email continuity access during outages via the Mimecast portal
* Policy-driven message hold and release for quarantined emails

Mimecast for Outlook is deployed alongside the Mimecast cloud email gateway in organisations that use Mimecast to secure and manage their Microsoft Exchange or Microsoft 365 email environments.
