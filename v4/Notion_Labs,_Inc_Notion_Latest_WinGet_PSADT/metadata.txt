**Notion** is a workspace application from Notion Labs that combines notes, documents, wikis, databases and project tracking in one place. The desktop app gives users their Notion workspaces in a dedicated window, with offline access to recently opened pages.

## Key Features

* Pages and sub-pages for notes, documents and team wikis
* Databases with table, board, calendar, list, gallery and timeline views
* Real-time collaboration, comments and @mentions
* Templates for meeting notes, project plans, task lists and more
* Notion AI for drafting, summarising and answering questions about your workspace
* Global search across every page and database you can access
* Sharing and permissions per page, including guests and external sharing
* Compatible with currently supported Windows operating systems
