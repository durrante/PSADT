**Adobe Acrobat Pro** is a professional PDF authoring and management application published by Adobe Inc. It is used across business, government, and education sectors for creating, editing, reviewing, and securing PDF documents.

## Key Features

* Create, edit, and export PDF documents
* OCR (optical character recognition) for scanned documents
* Electronic signatures and digital signing workflows
* Form creation and data collection
* Document redaction and protection
* Integration with Microsoft 365 and cloud storage services
* Comment, review, and approval workflows

Acrobat Pro is the standard tool for organisations that require full PDF editing capabilities beyond what free or reader-only applications provide.

> **Licensing note:** This package installs the Adobe Acrobat Pro unified installer. To access Pro features, users must sign in with an Adobe account that is assigned an Acrobat Pro licence. Users without a licence will still be able to use the application in a read-only capacity, equivalent to Adobe Acrobat Reader.
