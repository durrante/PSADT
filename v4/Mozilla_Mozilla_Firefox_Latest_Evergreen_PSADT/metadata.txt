**Mozilla Firefox** is a free, open-source web browser developed by Mozilla. It is built on Mozilla's own Gecko engine rather than Chromium, and is known for its privacy protections, standards support and extensive add-on library.

## Key Features

* Enhanced Tracking Protection to block trackers, cookies and fingerprinting by default
* Sync of bookmarks, passwords, history and open tabs across signed-in devices
* Firefox Add-ons library for extending and customising the browser
* Container tabs for keeping browsing sessions separate from each other
* Built-in password manager with breach alerts
* Reader mode, picture-in-picture video and a built-in PDF viewer
* Enterprise management through Group Policy and policies.json
* Compatible with currently supported Windows operating systems
